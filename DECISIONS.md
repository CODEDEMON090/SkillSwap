# SkillSwap Decision Points

## DP1 — Rejection
A declined booking remains visible to the client with a clear **Declined** status. The client can return to the marketplace and request another gig.

## DP2 — Double Booking
Multiple booking requests may remain Pending. Each request can independently be Accepted or Declined by the creator. This keeps the MVP simple and makes the decision explicit.

## DP3 — Discovery
When a search query exists, matching title/category/description/creator results are prioritized. Without a query, newest gigs appear first.

These are product decisions for the MVP and should be explained during the demo.
